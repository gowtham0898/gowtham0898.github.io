About

01

I'm a backend engineer who likes the unglamorous middle of a system — the part where a request becomes a queue message, a queue message becomes a database write, and a database write has to be right the first time. Most of my work sits in that space: scheduling engines, event pipelines, observability, and the databases behind them.

At Grasko, I work on the scheduling module of KanTime, a healthcare platform that coordinates clinician visits around patient needs and caregiver capacity. It's the kind of domain where the logic is genuinely complex — recurring visits, demand and frequency rules, reconciliation — and where a slow query or a dropped Kafka message has a real person waiting on the other end. That's shaped how I think about backend work: correctness and performance aren't separate concerns, they're the same job.
